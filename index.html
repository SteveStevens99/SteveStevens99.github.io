Prices

Global prices are in gold, local prices in dollars. Value, best market and best trade are worked out for you.

ItemGlobal GLocal $HeldValue GBest marketBest tradeList price GBuy at or below GSell at or above GAlertTrend

Value uses the best market after income tax. Best trade buys cheap, sells dear and takes income tax off the sale. Import and export taxes are not included. Buy at or below is the most you can pay in the cheaper market and still earn your profit margin after income tax by selling in the dearer one. Sell at or above is your average logged buy cost, or the cheapest market price if you have no log, plus your margin and income tax. Buy now shows when the cheapest market is at or below the buy price. Sell now shows when you hold units and the best market is at or above the sell price.
